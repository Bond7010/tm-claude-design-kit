# Prompt: Social Media Post

Use this prompt for quote cards, announcements, or motivational posts.

---

## Prompt template

```
Create a brand-compliant Toastmasters social media post [TYPE: quote card / announcement / role-call / congratulations].

Content:
- Main message: [TEXT]
- Club name: [CLUB NAME]
- Platform: [Facebook / Instagram / LinkedIn]

Brand requirements:
- Background: [choose one] Loyal Blue gradient (#004165 → #006094) | True Maroon gradient | White | Cool Gray
- Text on dark backgrounds: White or Happy Yellow #F2DF74
- Text on light backgrounds: Loyal Blue or True Maroon
- Headline font: Montserrat Bold
- No word art, no drop shadows on text
- Approved phrase (if applicable): [choose from approved-phrases.json]
- Logo placement: corner (bottom-right preferred) with clear space respected
```

---

## Background × text combinations

| Background | Headline | Body |
|---|---|---|
| Loyal Blue `#004165` | White | White |
| True Maroon `#772432` | White | White |
| Loyal Blue gradient | Happy Yellow `#F2DF74` | White |
| White | Loyal Blue `#004165` | Black |
| Cool Gray `#A9B2B1` | Loyal Blue | White |

## Common post types

- **Role-call** — list speaking roles for upcoming meeting (use Cool Gray or Blue bg)
- **Quote card** — member quote on gradient background, approved phrase as tagline
- **Congratulations** — award or achievement (True Maroon bg, white text, DTM/badge asset)
- **Recruitment** — "Find Your Voice" phrase, photo of engaged meeting, CTA with meeting time
