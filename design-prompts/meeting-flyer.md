# Prompt: Weekly Meeting Flyer

Use this prompt when generating a meeting flyer for social media or print.

---

## Prompt template

```
Create a brand-compliant Toastmasters meeting flyer for [PLATFORM: Facebook / Instagram / Print A4].

Club name: [CLUB NAME]
Meeting date: [DATE]
Meeting time: [TIME]
Location: [LOCATION or "Online via Zoom"]
Theme (optional): [THEME]
Guest info (optional): [e.g., "Guests welcome — first visit free"]

Brand requirements:
- Colors: Loyal Blue #004165, True Maroon #772432, Cool Gray #A9B2B1, Happy Yellow #F2DF74
- Headline font: Montserrat Bold or Black
- Body font: Source Sans 3
- Use one approved phrase: [choose from approved-phrases.json based on context]
- Logo: use white version on dark background, full-color on light background
- No custom taglines — only the chosen approved phrase
- Text must not cover any faces in photography
```

---

## Platform dimensions

| Platform | Size |
|---|---|
| Facebook post | 1200 × 630 px |
| Instagram square | 1080 × 1080 px |
| Instagram story | 1080 × 1920 px |
| Print A4 | 2480 × 3508 px at 300 dpi |

## Recommended approved phrase for meeting flyers

`"Find Your Voice"` — works for general meeting promotion and recruitment.
